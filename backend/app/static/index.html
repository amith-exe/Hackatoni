How Karshakan Lens Works

1. Below-Sea-Level Polder Hydrology

Kuttanad polders lie 1.5 to 2.5 meters below sea level. Salinity travels upstream through backwater canals, not in radial circles. Karshakan Lens models water movements through connected canal segments.

2. DBSCAN Spatial Clustering (2.0 km / 7 Days)

Using scikit-learn Haversine DBSCAN, nearby incidents within 2.0 km reported in the past 7 days are automatically clustered. Isolated noise is filtered out, while growing pest outbreaks trigger localized early warnings.

3. Community Peer Review & Officer Validation

A report begins as Unconfirmed. Once 2 neighboring farmers confirm the observation, it promotes to Community Confirmed. Krishi Bhavan officers can then grant Official Verification.

4. Zero Chemical Hallucinations

All action steps and spray guidelines are strictly grounded in Kerala Agricultural University (KAU) Package of Practices records. The app never uses an LLM to invent dosages.
